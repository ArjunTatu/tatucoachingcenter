Q12. Prime Cost and Provisional Cost

Prime Cost

Meaning

Prime Cost (PC) is the estimated amount included in the contract for supply of specific materials whose exact price is not known during estimation.

Features

  • Included as fixed allowance
  • Used for items like sanitary fittings and tiles
  • Final payment based on actual purchase cost
  • Contractor paid actual cost plus profit

Example

If sanitary fittings brand is not decided, a prime cost of Rs. 1,00,000 may be included in estimate and later adjusted.

Provisional Cost

Meaning

Provisional Cost (Provisional Sum) is an estimated amount included for work which cannot be fully defined during estimation.

Features

  • Used when work details are uncertain
  • Temporary amount in contract
  • Adjusted after actual execution
  • Includes material and labour cost

Example

Landscaping work may not be fully designed. A provisional sum of Rs. 2,00,000 may be included and adjusted after completion.

Difference

Prime Cost Provisional Cost
For supply of materials For complete work item
Material cost only Material and labour cost
Item selected later Work details not known
Adjusted by purchase cost Adjusted by actual work done

Conclusion

Prime cost is an allowance for supply of materials whose price is uncertain, while provisional cost is an estimated amount for work not fully defined at the time of estimation.
