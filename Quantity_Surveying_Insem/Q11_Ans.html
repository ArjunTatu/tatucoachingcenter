Q11. Five Items with Mode of Measurement

Introduction

In quantity surveying, each construction item is measured in a specific unit such as cubic meter, square meter or number.

Item of Work Unit Mode of Measurement
Earthwork Excavation m³ Length × Breadth × Depth
Plain Cement Concrete m³ Length × Breadth × Thickness
Brick Masonry m³ Length × Breadth × Height
Plastering m² Length × Height
Flooring m² Length × Breadth
Reinforcement Steel kg / tonne Weight of steel used
Doors & Windows No. / m² As per design size

Conclusion

Different construction items are measured in cubic meter, square meter, running meter, kilogram or number depending on the nature of work. Proper measurement ensures accurate quantity calculation.
