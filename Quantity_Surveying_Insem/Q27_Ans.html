Q27. Role of Arbitrator in Civil Engineering Works

Introduction

In civil engineering contracts disputes may arise between contractor and owner regarding payment, delay or quality of work.

Meaning of Arbitrator

An arbitrator is a neutral person appointed to settle disputes between two parties outside the court.

Role of Arbitrator

  • Settlement of disputes related to payment, delay and extra work
  • Examination of contract clauses
  • Hearing arguments from both parties
  • Verification of records such as bills and drawings
  • Site inspection if required
  • Giving final written decision called Arbitral Award

Example

If contractor claims extra payment for additional excavation and department refuses, the matter is referred to arbitrator for decision.

Conclusion

The arbitrator plays an important role in resolving disputes quickly and fairly, avoiding lengthy court procedures.
