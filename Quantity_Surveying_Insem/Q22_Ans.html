Q22. Registration Procedure for Contractor

Introduction

Before participating in government tenders, a contractor must register with concerned department such as PWD.

Purpose of Registration

  • To verify technical qualification
  • To check financial capacity
  • To ensure experience
  • To maintain quality and transparency

Registration Procedure

1. Submission of Application

  • Contractor submits application form
  • Application submitted to department

2. Submission of Required Documents

  • Educational qualification certificate
  • Experience certificate
  • Financial statements
  • Income tax clearance
  • GST registration
  • PAN card
  • Bank solvency certificate

3. Payment of Registration Fees

  • Fees paid according to contractor class

4. Verification of Documents

  • Technical capacity checked
  • Financial strength verified
  • Experience examined

5. Classification of Contractor

  • Class I – High value works
  • Class II – Medium value works
  • Class III – Small works

6. Issue of Registration Certificate

  • Certificate issued after approval
  • Valid for specific period
  • Contractor allowed to participate in tenders

Example

A contractor who wants to execute works up to ₹50 lakh must register under suitable class by submitting documents and paying registration fees.

Conclusion

Contractor registration ensures that only capable and qualified contractors are allowed to execute government construction works.
