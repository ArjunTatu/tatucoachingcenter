Approximate Estimate of Town Hall Building

(Plinth Area Method)

Given Data

  • Plinth area = 1500 m²
  • Plinth area rate = Rs. 3200 per m²
  • Special architectural treatment = 2%
  • Water supply & sanitary installation = 5%
  • Electric installation = 14%
  • Other services = 6%
  • Contingencies = 3%
  • Supervision charges = 8%

Step 1: Calculate Building Cost

Building Cost = Plinth Area × Plinth Area Rate
= 1500 × 3200
= Rs. 48,00,000

Step 2: Add Special Architectural Treatment (2%)

2% of 48,00,000 = 96,000
Subtotal = 48,00,000 + 96,000
= Rs. 48,96,000

Step 3: Add Water Supply & Sanitary (5%)

5% of 48,00,000 = 2,40,000
Subtotal = 48,96,000 + 2,40,000
= Rs. 51,36,000

Step 4: Add Electric Installation (14%)

14% of 48,00,000 = 6,72,000
Subtotal = 51,36,000 + 6,72,000
= Rs. 58,08,000

Step 5: Add Other Services (6%)

6% of 48,00,000 = 2,88,000
Subtotal = 58,08,000 + 2,88,000
= Rs. 60,96,000

Step 6: Add Contingencies (3%)

3% of 60,96,000 = 1,82,880
Subtotal = 60,96,000 + 1,82,880
= Rs. 62,78,880

Step 7: Add Supervision Charges (8%)

8% of 62,78,880 = 5,02,310

Final Estimated Cost

Total Cost = 62,78,880 + 5,02,310

Approximate Estimated Cost of Town Hall
= Rs. 67,81,190

≈ Rs. 67.8 Lakhs
