Q24. Five Conditions of Contract Related to Execution of Work

Introduction

A contract contains various conditions to ensure proper execution of construction work. These conditions define rights and responsibilities of both contractor and owner.

Important Conditions

1. Time of Completion

  • Contractor must complete work within specified time
  • Time schedule mentioned in agreement
  • Delay may attract penalty

Example: Work must be completed within 12 months.

2. Quality of Materials and Workmanship

  • Materials must follow specifications
  • Engineer can reject poor quality work
  • Standard codes must be followed

Example: Cement must be approved brand.

3. Payment Terms

  • Payment made based on measured work
  • Running bills paid periodically
  • Final bill paid after completion

4. Security Deposit

  • Contractor deposits percentage of contract value
  • Acts as security for proper performance
  • Released after defects liability period

5. Extra Items and Variations

  • Extra work must be approved in writing
  • Rates decided according to contract
  • Payment made accordingly

Example: Extra foundation depth due to weak soil.

Conclusion

Conditions of contract ensure timely completion, quality work and fair payment. They help smooth execution of civil engineering projects.
