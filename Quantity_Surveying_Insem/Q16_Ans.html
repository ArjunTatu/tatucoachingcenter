Q16. PWD Procedure for Execution of Minor Works

Introduction

Public Works Department (PWD) follows a systematic procedure for execution of minor works such as small buildings, repairs, culverts and maintenance works.

Procedure

1. Preparation of Estimate

  • Site inspection
  • Rough estimate preparation
  • Detailed estimate with quantities and rates
  • Checking and approval

2. Administrative Approval

  • Approval of necessity of work
  • Budget provision confirmed

3. Technical Sanction

  • Verification of drawings
  • Checking quantities and rates
  • Technical approval by engineer

4. Tendering Process

  • Tender notice prepared
  • Tenders invited
  • Tenders compared
  • Lowest suitable bidder selected

5. Issue of Work Order

  • Work order issued
  • Agreement signed
  • Time limit specified

6. Execution of Work

  • Site handed over
  • Work executed as per drawings
  • Supervision by PWD staff

7. Measurement of Work

  • Measurements recorded in Measurement Book
  • Running bills prepared

8. Payment of Bills

  • Bills verified
  • Payment made
  • Final bill prepared

9. Completion

  • Inspection by engineer
  • Defects corrected
  • Completion certificate issued
