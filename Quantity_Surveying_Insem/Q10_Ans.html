Q10. Estimating Irrigation Type Civil Works

Introduction

Irrigation works include canals, dams, weirs, barrages, distributaries and other hydraulic structures. While preparing estimates for irrigation works, several technical and site-related factors must be considered.

Site Investigation

  • Type of soil
  • Ground water level
  • Nature of foundation
  • Topography of area

These factors affect excavation and foundation cost.

Type of Structure

  • Canal
  • Dam
  • Weir
  • Barrage
  • Aqueduct

Earthwork Calculation

  • Large quantity of excavation
  • Embankment work
  • Cutting and filling
  • Shrinkage and compaction allowance

Earthwork forms the major part of irrigation project cost.

Lead and Lift

  • Distance of earth transport
  • Height of lifting materials

Availability of Materials

  • Source of sand
  • Source of stone
  • Availability of cement
  • Transportation facilities

Water Control Arrangements

  • Temporary diversion channels
  • Dewatering arrangements
  • Cofferdams

Hydraulic Design Considerations

  • Discharge capacity
  • Slope of canal
  • Lining requirement
  • Seepage control

Structures in Canal

  • Cross drainage works
  • Culverts
  • Regulators
  • Head works

Machinery and Equipment

  • Excavators
  • Pumps
  • Concrete mixers

Contingencies and Supervision

  • Provision for unforeseen work
  • Work charged establishment
  • Supervision charges

Conclusion

Proper consideration of soil conditions, earthwork, lead and lift, hydraulic design and material availability ensures accurate estimation of irrigation works.
