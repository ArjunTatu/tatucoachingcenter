Approximate Estimate of Hospital Building

(Service Unit Method – Bed Method)

Given Data

  • Number of beds = 135
  • Cost of 125 bed hospital = Rs. 26,00,000
  • Rise in construction cost = 20%
  • Provision for water supply, drainage & electrification = 12%

Step 1: Cost per Bed

Cost per bed = 26,00,000 / 125
= Rs. 20,800

Step 2: Add 20% Increase

Increase = 20% of 20,800 = 4,160
New cost per bed = 20,800 + 4,160
= Rs. 24,960

Step 3: Cost for 135 Beds

Construction Cost = 24,960 × 135
= Rs. 33,69,600

Step 4: Add 12% Services

12% of 33,69,600 = Rs. 4,04,352

Final Estimated Cost

Total Cost = 33,69,600 + 4,04,352

Approximate Estimated Cost of Hospital
= Rs. 37,73,952

≈ Rs. 37.74 Lakhs
