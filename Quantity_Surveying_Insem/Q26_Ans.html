Q26. Contract and Objectives

Introduction

In civil engineering works, an agreement between owner and contractor is required before starting construction. This agreement is called a contract.

Meaning

A contract is a legally binding agreement between two or more parties to perform certain work under specified terms and conditions.

Essential Elements of Contract

  • Offer
  • Acceptance
  • Lawful consideration
  • Free consent
  • Competent parties
  • Lawful object

Objectives of Contract

  • Clear Understanding – Defines scope and responsibilities
  • Legal Protection – Protects rights of both parties
  • Time and Cost Control – Specifies completion period and cost
  • Quality Assurance – Ensures work as per standards
  • Dispute Resolution – Provides arbitration clause
  • Financial Security – Includes security deposit and payment terms

Example

If PWD awards a ₹1 crore bridge project, the contract will specify scope of work, time of completion, payment terms and penalties.

Conclusion

A contract ensures smooth execution of construction work and avoids disputes by clearly defining duties and responsibilities.
