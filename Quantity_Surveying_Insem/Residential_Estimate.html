Approximate Estimate of Residential Building

(Cost Index Method)

Given Data

  • Built up area = 350 sq.m
  • Cost of 300 sq.m building = Rs. 16,00,000
  • Rise in construction cost = 24%
  • Provision for water supply, drainage & electrification = 15%

Step 1: Cost per sq.m

Cost per sq.m = 16,00,000 / 300
= Rs. 5,333.33

Step 2: Add 24% Increase

Increase = 24% of 5,333.33
= Rs. 1,280
New cost per sq.m = 6,613.33

Step 3: Construction Cost for 350 sq.m

6,613.33 × 350
= Rs. 23,14,665

Step 4: Add 15% Services

15% of 23,14,665 = Rs. 3,47,200

Final Estimated Cost

Total Cost = 23,14,665 + 3,47,200

Approximate Estimated Cost of Residential Building
= Rs. 26,61,865

≈ Rs. 26.62 Lakhs
