Q17. Arbitration

Introduction

In construction projects, disputes may arise between contractor and department regarding payment, delay or quality of work.

Meaning

Arbitration is a method of resolving disputes outside the court by appointing a neutral third person called an arbitrator.

The arbitrator hears both parties and gives a decision called an Arbitral Award.

Need of Arbitration

  • Quick settlement of disputes
  • Less expensive than court cases
  • Technical expertise of arbitrator
  • Confidential process
  • Legally binding decision
  • Maintains professional relationship

Example

If contractor claims extra payment for additional work and department refuses, the matter can be referred to arbitration instead of court.

Conclusion

Arbitration provides quick and fair settlement of disputes in civil engineering contracts without lengthy court procedures.
