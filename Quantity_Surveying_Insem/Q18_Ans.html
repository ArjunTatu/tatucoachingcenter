Q18. Powers of Arbitrator

Introduction

An arbitrator is a neutral person appointed to resolve disputes between parties in a contract.

Powers of Arbitrator

  • Power to hear both parties
  • Power to call documents and records
  • Power to take evidence and examine witnesses
  • Power to inspect construction site
  • Power to appoint technical experts
  • Power to decide disputes related to payment, delay and extra work
  • Power to grant relief or compensation
  • Power to issue final arbitral award

Conclusion

The arbitrator has sufficient legal powers to investigate and settle disputes fairly. His decision helps avoid long court cases and ensures speedy justice.
