Q21. Tender and Example

Introduction

In construction projects, work is usually not given directly to a contractor. Contractors are invited to submit their rates through tendering.

Meaning of Tender

A tender is a written offer submitted by a contractor to execute a specific work at specified rates within specified time under certain conditions.

Definition

Tender is an offer made by a contractor in response to a tender notice to execute a work according to drawings, specifications and conditions at quoted rates.

Important Features

  • It is a written document
  • Submitted before last date and time
  • Includes quoted rates
  • Includes Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
  • Based on drawings and specifications
  • Legally binding after acceptance

Documents Included in Tender

  • Notice Inviting Tender
  • General conditions of contract
  • Special conditions
  • Bill of Quantities (BOQ)
  • Form of agreement
  • Specifications and drawings

Example

PWD invites tender for construction of school building costing ₹80 lakh.

  • Contractor A quotes ₹78 lakh
  • Contractor B quotes ₹82 lakh
  • Contractor C quotes ₹76 lakh

Contractor C being the lowest suitable bidder is selected.

Importance of Tender

  • Ensures competition
  • Provides transparency
  • Helps in selecting qualified contractor
  • Protects public funds

Conclusion

Tender is a formal written offer by contractor to execute construction work at specified rates and conditions ensuring fairness and transparency.
