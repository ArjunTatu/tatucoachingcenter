Q25. Tender Notice and Essential Contents

Introduction

Before starting any construction work, contractors are invited to submit bids through a document called a Tender Notice.

Meaning

Tender Notice is a public announcement issued by a department inviting contractors to submit tenders for a specific work.

Purpose

  • Inform contractors about proposed work
  • Ensure transparency
  • Invite competitive bids
  • Select suitable contractor

Essential Contents

  • Name of Department
  • Name and Description of Work
  • Estimated Cost of Work
  • Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)
  • Cost of Tender Form
  • Time of Completion
  • Eligibility Criteria
  • Date and Time of Submission
  • Contact Details

Example

PWD issues tender notice for construction of hospital costing ₹2 crore with EMD of ₹2 lakh and completion time of 18 months.

Conclusion

Tender notice is an important document that initiates the tendering process and ensures transparency and fair competition in civil engineering works.
